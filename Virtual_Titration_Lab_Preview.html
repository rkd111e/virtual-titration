CANDIDATE QUESTION PAPER

Question 1 — Titration

CAMBRIDGE-STYLE PRACTICE · ORIGINAL SIMULATED QUESTION

FA 1 is aqueous hydrochloric acid, HCl, of concentration 0.100 mol dm⁻³.

FA 2 is aqueous sodium hydroxide, NaOH, of unknown concentration.

Procedure

  1. Fill the burette with FA 1 and record the initial burette reading.
  2. Using a pipette and filler, transfer 25.0 cm³ of FA 2 into a conical flask.
  3. Add a few drops of methyl orange indicator.
  4. Titrate with FA 1 while swirling, until a lasting orange endpoint is observed.
  5. Record the final burette reading and determine the titre.
  6. Carry out a rough titration followed by sufficient accurate titrations to obtain concordant values.

Results and calculations

Record initial and final readings to the nearest 0.05 cm³. Use concordant accurate titres, within 0.10 cm³, to calculate a mean titre to two decimal places. Deduce the concentration of FA 2 in mol dm⁻³.

Safety: treat both solutions as laboratory reagents; in a physical laboratory, wear appropriate eye protection and use a pipette filler. This simulator starts with clean, prepared apparatus.
